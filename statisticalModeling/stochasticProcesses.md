# Stochastic Processes

## Overview
A stochastic process is a collection of random variables indexed by time, describing how a quantity evolves under uncertainty. Markov chains, random walks, and Gaussian processes are common examples, and a stochastic matrix describes the transition probabilities of a Markov chain. In robotics they model sensor noise, uncertain motion, and disturbances, and they underlie state estimation methods such as the Kalman filter and particle filters.
