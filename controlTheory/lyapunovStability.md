# Lyapunov Stability

## Overview
Lyapunov stability gives a way to prove that a system settles to an equilibrium without solving its dynamics: find an energy-like function that is positive and always decreasing along trajectories. It is the foundation of nonlinear control analysis. In robotics, Lyapunov functions are used to prove that tracking controllers, adaptive controllers, and learned policies are stable, and to design controllers (control Lyapunov functions) that guarantee convergence.
