# Dynamic Programming

## Overview
Dynamic programming solves sequential decision problems by breaking them into smaller subproblems and using Bellman's principle of optimality, which says an optimal policy's remaining decisions are also optimal. It is the basis of value iteration and policy iteration, of the optimal control methods behind LQR, and of many reinforcement learning algorithms. In robotics it is used for planning, such as finding least-cost paths and optimal policies in discrete problems.
