# Phase Portraits

## Overview
A phase portrait plots a system's trajectories in state space (for example position against velocity), showing equilibria, their stability, and the overall flow without solving the equations. They are the main visual tool for understanding second-order and nonlinear systems. In robotics they are used to understand pendulum-like systems, to see how a controller changes the closed-loop behavior, and to reason about limit cycles in walking and oscillating systems.
