# Optimal Control

## Overview
Optimal control chooses control inputs that minimize a cost (such as energy, time, or tracking error) subject to the system dynamics and constraints. It covers methods such as LQR, the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation, and Pontryagin's principle. In robotics it is used for trajectory optimization, for computing efficient and smooth motions, and as the basis of model predictive control and many reinforcement learning methods.
