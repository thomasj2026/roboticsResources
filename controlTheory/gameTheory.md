# Game Theory

## Overview
Game theory studies how decision makers choose actions when the outcome depends on what the others do. Concepts such as Nash equilibria and zero-sum games are used to model competition and cooperation. In robotics it is used for multi-robot coordination, interaction with humans and other vehicles (for example in autonomous driving), and for robust and adversarial control, where disturbances are treated as an opponent.
