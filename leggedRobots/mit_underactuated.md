# MIT Underactuated Robotics

## Overview
Underactuated robots have fewer actuators than degrees of freedom, as with legged robots, flying robots, and swinging or balancing systems. They cannot follow arbitrary trajectories, so they need planning and control methods that exploit the dynamics instead of cancelling them. This area covers trajectory optimization, Lyapunov methods, and reinforcement learning applied to legged locomotion and manipulation.

## Sources
- [Underactuated Robotics (MIT, Russ Tedrake)](https://underactuated.csail.mit.edu/index.html): a free online textbook and course that covers robot dynamics, modeling, and the theory behind legged robots, along with optimal control, trajectory optimization, and reinforcement learning, with interactive notebooks.
