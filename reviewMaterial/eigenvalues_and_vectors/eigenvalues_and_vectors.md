# Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

## Overview
An eigenvector of a matrix is a direction that the matrix only stretches, and the eigenvalue is the stretch factor. They show up throughout robotics and controls: the eigenvalues of a linearized system's matrix determine whether it is stable, they give the natural modes and time constants of the system, and they are used in principal component analysis and in analyzing covariance matrices for state estimation.
