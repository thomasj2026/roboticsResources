# Nonlinear Dynamics

## Overview
Nonlinear dynamics studies systems whose behavior cannot be described by linear equations, including equilibria, stability, bifurcations, limit cycles, and chaos. Most real robots (pendulums, legged systems, vehicles) are nonlinear, so these concepts are the background for nonlinear control, phase portraits, and Lyapunov analysis.
