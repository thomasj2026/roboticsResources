# Diffusion Models

## Overview
Diffusion models are generative models that learn to produce data by gradually removing noise from random samples. In robotics they are used for diffusion policies, which generate sequences of robot actions from observations for manipulation and imitation learning, and for generating images, trajectories, and motion plans.
