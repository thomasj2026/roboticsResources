# Tools

## Overview
MATLAB and Simulink are widely used in robotics and controls for modeling, simulation, controller design, and data analysis. The MATLAB Tech Talks series from MathWorks explains the underlying concepts with short videos, and it is a good complement to coursework in controls and estimation.
