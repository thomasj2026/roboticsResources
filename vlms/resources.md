# Vision-Language Models (VLMs)

## Overview
Vision-language models take images and text together and produce text, so they can describe a scene, answer questions about it, or follow instructions that refer to what a camera sees. In robotics they are used for scene understanding, open-vocabulary object recognition, and language-conditioned manipulation and navigation, where a robot follows natural-language commands.
